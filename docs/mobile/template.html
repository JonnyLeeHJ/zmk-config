Everyday shortcuts

Tap Tab for Tab. Hold it for 200 ms before pressing another key for Ctrl shortcuts. J and K are ordinary letters.

On Mac, GUI is Command and Alt is Option. On Windows, GUI is Windows and Right Alt may act as AltGr, depending on your keyboard language.

For Caps Word, hold Lower and tap the right outer home-row key. Release Lower, then type. Space ends Caps Word; underscores keep it active.

Reading the layers

Lower: hold the left outer thumb. Raise: hold the right outer bottom key. Adjust: hold both together.

On Adjust, inherited bindings resolve through Raise, then Lower, then Base. These are reference buttons: tapping them here never sends a command to your keyboard.
